# Power-BI-Projects
Power BI dashboards showcasing data analysis and business intelligence projects.
Executive Analytics Portfolio: Enterprise HR & E-Commerce Business Intelligence
This repository contains interactive Power BI dashboards designed to drive data-informed decision-making across enterprise human resources and digital retail operations.

🎯 Project Objectives
1. Human Resources (HR) Attrition Analytics
Attrition Tracking: Identify headcount, turnover rate, and average employee age/tenure across departments.

Demographic Breakdown: Map employee attrition by age groups, education field, gender, and salary slab.

Satisfaction & Engagement: Evaluate job satisfaction levels across specific roles (Sales, R&D, HR) to pinpoint retention risks.

2. E-Commerce Sales & Revenue Analytics
Revenue Metrics: Track total sales, profit margins, unit orders, and average order values (AOV).

Performance Breakdown: Monitor top-selling product categories, regional performance, and monthly growth trends.

Customer Behavior: Analyze sales channels, payment preferences, and repeat customer retention patterns.

🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools
Business Intelligence: Microsoft Power BI (Desktop & Service)

Data Transformation: Power Query / M Code

Data Modeling: DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) for explicit measures, metrics, and KPI card logic

Data Source: Relational CSV Datasets (HR Records & Online Sales Transactions)

📊 Key Insights & Findings
HR Analytics Dashboard Insights
Turnover Hotspot: Attrition is concentrated among employees aged 25–34, with Life Sciences and Medical educational backgrounds driving the largest total volume of departures.

Departmental Variance: The Research & Development department accounts for the highest raw count of active employees and departures, whereas Sales Executives and Laboratory Technicians experience the highest rate of job dissatisfaction.

Salary Correlation: Employees in lower salary brackets show significantly higher voluntary attrition, highlighting compensation and career progression as primary drivers of employee turnover.

E-Commerce Analytics Dashboard Insights
Revenue Drivers: Core inventory categories generate over 60% of total profit margins, indicating strong revenue reliance on top-tier items.

Fulfillment Impact: Seasonal sales surges directly correlate with longer fulfillment times, which negatively impact customer repeat purchase rates.
